Meet The Founder • Masarh to Arrah

From Viral Desi Comedy to Arrah’s Signature Fashion Hub.

Shubham Singh—widely celebrated online as Shubham Singh Vines—is a digital creator and entrepreneur rooted in Masarh, Bhojpur (Arrah, Bihar). Speaking Hindi, Bhojpuri, and English, Shubham built a devoted nationwide audience through relatable desi-style comedy skits, Chhath Puja specials, and authentic village-lifestyle storytelling.

Turning his creative momentum into local impact, Shubham launched Vines Fashion in Maharana Pratap Nagar (Near Sankat Mochan), Arrah—named directly after his creator moniker—with a clear mission: bringing trendy, metro-quality, and budget-friendly men's and women's fashion right to Bhojpur’s doorstep.
